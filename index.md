## Cured at a price — the cure itself takes something permanent

- **penile-cancer** — curable, **harsh** · catastrophic, 0.55 of treated · fraction-of-treated with permanent anatomical, urinary or sexual loss — reasoned, and lower where disease is caught early and specialist services exist
- **head-neck-cancer** — curable, **harsh** · catastrophic, 0.25 of treated · fraction-of-treated (**catastrophic** permanent harm — loss of voice after laryngectomy, long-term feeding-tube dependence, osteoradionecrosis, disfiguring resection) — a reasoned estimate, concentrated in the locally advanced stratum. **The layer beneath it is much larger and this field cannot hold two.** Permanent xerostomia is close to universal after radiotherapy to these fields, and dysphagia of some degree affects a majority — `major` harm at perhaps 0.60, sitting under a `catastrophic` harm at 0.25. SCHEMA.md says severity describes the harm *when it happens* and incidence carries how often, which is right and means **a treatment with two distinct permanent tolls at two severities can only report one of them**
- **mycetoma** — curable, **harsh** · catastrophic, 0.15 of treated · fraction-of-treated (amputation) — a reasoned estimate blended across both forms; far higher within eumycetoma and near zero within actinomycetoma
- **sickle-cell** — curable, **harsh** · catastrophic, 0.99 of treated · fraction-of-treated (gene therapy recipients undergoing myeloablation)
- **crohns** — managed, **harsh** · catastrophic, 0.5 of treated · fraction-of-patients (major abdominal surgery within ten years)
- **pleomorphic-adenoma** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.025 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent facial weakness, primary surgery)
- **thyroid-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.05 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent harm — hypoparathyroidism, recurrent laryngeal nerve injury, salivary damage) — low per patient, and **the denominator is inflated by hundreds of thousands of operations that should not have happened**
- **uterine-fibroids** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.45 of treated · fraction-of-treated left with a permanent consequence of treatment — dominated by hysterectomy, and including myomectomy scarring and embolisation effects on ovarian reserve. A reasoned estimate
- **benign-prostatic-hyperplasia** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.7 of treated · fraction-of-surgically-treated left with a permanent consequence, almost entirely retrograde ejaculation — a reasoned estimate. Newer ejaculation-preserving procedures reduce it substantially and are not yet the majority of what is done
- **coccidioidomycosis** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.2 of treated · fraction-of-treated with significant drug toxicity or a management-altering interaction over a prolonged course — reasoned
- **maternal-haemorrhage** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.02 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent harm — chiefly emergency hysterectomy) — a reasoned estimate, and several times higher in settings without blood or a full treatment ladder
- **vulvar-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.35 of treated · fraction-of-treated with permanent lymphoedema, sexual or urinary consequence — reasoned, and far lower where sentinel node biopsy is available
- **anal-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.4 of treated · fraction-of-treated with permanent bowel, sexual or pelvic dysfunction — reasoned, and high because the radiation field contains the sphincter
- **uterine-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.35 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent harm — infertility, surgical menopause, lymphoedema, radiation sequelae, surgical complications of obesity) — a reasoned estimate
- **breast-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.35 of treated · fraction-of-treated (any permanent sequela of treatment)
- **prostate-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.45 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent harm — erectile dysfunction, incontinence, bowel toxicity, or the metabolic and skeletal consequences of androgen deprivation) — a reasoned estimate over men receiving definitive treatment, and it EXCLUDES those on surveillance, which is the point of surveillance
- **neonatal-conditions** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.05 of treated · fraction-of-intensively-treated (permanent harm — chiefly retinopathy of prematurity and bronchopulmonary dysplasia) — a reasoned estimate, higher in units with monitoring gaps than in either the best-equipped or the least-equipped settings
- **childhood-all** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.55 of treated · fraction-of-survivors (at least one permanent treatment-attributable health condition by middle age) — drawn from long-term survivorship cohort findings; the severe or life-threatening fraction is roughly half of that again
- **uveal-melanoma** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.5 of treated · fraction-of-treated (loss of the eye, or of useful vision in it, attributable to treatment) — near-certain for enucleation, and a majority of irradiated eyes over five to ten years
- **retinopathy-of-prematurity** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.8 of treated · fraction-of-treated with permanent consequences of treatment — chiefly peripheral field loss and high myopia after laser — a reasoned estimate, high because the ablation is the mechanism rather than a complication
- **renal-cell-carcinoma** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.3 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent harm — reduced renal function after nephrectomy, chronic TKI toxicity, immune-related endocrine failure) — a reasoned estimate across the treated population
- **echinococcosis** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.1 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent surgical or drug-related sequelae) — a reasoned estimate, concentrated in those undergoing resection
- **nasopharyngeal-carcinoma** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.5 of treated · fraction-of-treated (permanent harm — xerostomia, hearing loss, hypothyroidism or hypopituitarism, fibrosis) — a reasoned estimate, and high because most of these are near-inevitable consequences of adequate dose rather than complications of it. Catastrophic harm, chiefly temporal lobe necrosis and disabling cranial neuropathy, is a small fraction of it
- **vaginal-cancer** — curable, **costly** · major, 0.45 of treated · fraction-of-treated with permanent vaginal, urinary or bowel consequence — reasoned, and high because the radiation field cannot avoid the surrounding organs
